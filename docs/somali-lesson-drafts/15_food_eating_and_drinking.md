# Lesson 15: Food Practice: Eating and Drinking

Module: Daily Basics
Suggested skills: `food_eating_drinking`, `daily_food_production`
Source material: `Chatbot_1.pdf`, pages 8-10 and 13

Focus
Reinforce food and drink by producing full sentences about eating, drinking, liking, and wanting.

Why this lesson exists
Food vocabulary already exists in the live course, but the transcript shows the learner needs sentence-production reps: "I eat," "I eat rice," "I like coffee," and "I want to drink tea."

Prerequisites
- Food and Drink
- Present shapes in common verbs
- `waan` vs `waxaan`

Core vocabulary
- `cunayaa` - eat / am eating
- `cabbo` - drink, after `inaan`
- `bariis` - rice
- `moos` - banana
- `shaah` - tea
- `bun` - coffee
- `qado` - lunch / midday meal
- `wax` - something
- `cunto` - food

Phrases
- `Waan cuna.` - I eat.
- `Waan cunayaa.` - I am eating now.
- `Waxaan cunayaa bariis.` - I am eating rice.
- `Waxaan cunayaa moos.` - I am eating a banana.
- `Waxaan cunayaa qado.` - I am eating lunch.
- `Waan jeclahay bun.` - I like coffee.
- `Waxaan rabaa shaah.` - I want tea.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan wax cuno.` - I want to eat something.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan cabbo shaah.` - I want to drink tea.

Mini-dialogue
- A: `Ma doonaysaa shaah?` - Do you want tea?
- B: `Haa, waxaan rabaa shaah.` - Yes, I want tea.
- A: `Ma rabtaa wax aad cunto?` - Do you want something to eat?
- B: `Haa, waxaan rabaa inaan wax cuno.` - Yes, I want to eat something.
