# Lesson 12: Write: A Short Self-Introduction

Module: Greetings and Foundations
Suggested skills: `write_short_self_introduction`, `foundations_writing_extension`
Source material: `Chatbot_1.pdf`, pages 2-5

Focus
Help the learner produce a tiny paragraph about themself using five reliable sentence frames.

Why this lesson exists
The transcript shows the learner can assemble useful pieces but benefits from a structured frame before free writing. This should feel like building a short profile, not passing a grammar exam.

Prerequisites
- Introductions
- Family members
- Simple descriptions
- `waan` and `waxaan` seen passively

Core vocabulary
- `magacaygu` - my name
- `waxaan` - I, used before a longer predicate
- `joogaa` - am staying / located
- `waan` - I, used in a short direct sentence
- `ahay` - am
- `arday` - student
- `macallin` - teacher
- `shaqaale` - worker / employee
- `jecelahay` - like
- `saaxiibaday` - my friends
- `qoyskaygu` - my family

Phrases
- `Magacaygu waa Peter.` - My name is Peter.
- `Waxaan joogaa Copenhagen.` - I am in Copenhagen.
- `Waan ahay arday.` - I am a student.
- `Waan jeclahay kubbadda cagta.` - I like football.
- `Saaxiibaday waxay joogaan magaalada.` - My friends are in the city.
- `Qoyskaygu waxay joogaan Denmark.` - My family is in Denmark.

Writing task
Write four or five short Somali sentences about yourself.

Required frames
- `Magacaygu waa ...`
- `Waxaan joogaa ...`
- `Waan ahay ...`
- `Waan jeclahay ...`
- `Qoyskaygu ...` or `Saaxiibaday ...`

Sample answer
`Magacaygu waa Peter. Waxaan joogaa Copenhagen. Waan ahay arday. Waan jeclahay kubbadda cagta. Qoyskaygu waxay joogaan Denmark.`

Feedback priorities
- Sentence completeness before advanced naturalness.
- Correct `Waxaan joogaa ...` before correcting smaller style choices.
- Preserve the learner's chosen personal content when possible.
- Keep `Waan ahay arday` acceptable here even if later lessons introduce more idiomatic alternatives.

Notes for YAML
- Make this a `write` skill rather than a normal phrase drill.
- Grade for short complete sentences, not stylistic perfection.
- Good feedback targets: missing `waxaan`, word order, and `joogaa` vowel length.
- Keep names and cities variable where possible.
- The sample answer should be support, not a template the learner is forced to copy exactly.
