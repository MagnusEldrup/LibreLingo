# Somali Transcript-Derived Lesson Drafts

These drafts are a second-pass refinement of 10 lessons based on `Chatbot_1.pdf`, `Chatbot_2.pdf`, and the existing Somali course progression.

They are not live app content yet. They are planning drafts for future conversion into course data and challenge files.

Draft sequence:
- Lesson 11: Conversation: Where Are You?
- Lesson 12: Write: A Short Self-Introduction
- Lesson 13: Grammar: Waan vs Waxaan
- Lesson 14: Grammar: Present Shapes in Common Verbs
- Lesson 15: Food Practice: Eating and Drinking
- Lesson 16: Grammar: Waxaan Rabaa Inaan...
- Lesson 17: Past vs Present: Eat and Go
- Lesson 18: Questions: Answer in Full Sentences
- Lesson 19: Oral Drill: Translate Quickly
- Lesson 20: Food Detail: Qado and Qadada

Quality goals:
- Complement the existing course rather than duplicate it.
- Keep each lesson focused on one learner problem.
- Preserve a gentle progression from recognition to controlled production.
- Avoid turning beginner rules into overbroad grammar claims.
- Keep future YAML conversion notes close to each draft.
