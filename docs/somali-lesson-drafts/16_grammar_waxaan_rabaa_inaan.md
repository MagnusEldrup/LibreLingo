# Lesson 16: Grammar: Waxaan Rabaa Inaan...

Module: Daily Basics
Suggested skills: `waxaan_rabaa_inaan`, `want_to_do`
Source material: `Chatbot_1.pdf`, pages 7-10 and 13-14

Focus
Teach the high-frequency pattern `Waxaan rabaa inaan...` for saying what the learner wants to do.

Why this lesson exists
The learner repeatedly tried to express wanting, liking, and intended action. This pattern is a high-payoff bridge from simple present sentences into more natural needs and plans.

Prerequisites
- `waan` vs `waxaan`
- Food production
- Places and directions

Core vocabulary
- `rabaa` - want
- `inaan` - that I / to
- `cuno` - eat
- `cabbo` - drink
- `barto` - learn
- `aado` - go
- `akhriyo` - read
- `ciyaaro` - play
- `af Soomaali` - Somali language

Pattern
`Waxaan rabaa inaan` + action.

Phrases
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan cuno.` - I want to eat.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan wax cuno.` - I want to eat something.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan cabbo shaah.` - I want to drink tea.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan barto af Soomaali.` - I want to learn Somali.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan aado suuqa.` - I want to go to the market.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan akhriyo buug.` - I want to read a book.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan ciyaaro.` - I want to play.

Contrast
- `Waan cunayaa.` - I am eating.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan cuno.` - I want to eat.
- `Waxaan cunayaa bariis.` - I am eating rice.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan cuno bariis.` - I want to eat rice.

Common confusions to prevent
- `Waan jeclahay bun.` - I like coffee.
- `Waxaan rabaa bun.` - I want coffee.
- `Waxaan rabaa inaan cabbo bun.` - I want to drink coffee.

Notes for YAML
- Good as a grammar-table lesson with sentence completion.
- Keep the explanation short; let repeated examples teach the pattern.
- This prepares learners for later planning and future-tense content.
- Include `af Soomaali` because the transcript included wanting to learn Somali.
- Use one action family at a time: eat/drink first, then learn/go/read.
- Do not mix in future `doonaa` in this lesson.
