# Lesson 11: Conversation: Where Are You?

Module: Greetings and Foundations
Suggested skills: `conversation_where_are_you`, `current_location`
Source material: `Chatbot_1.pdf`, pages 1 and 5-6

Focus
Extend introductions into a short exchange about where someone currently is.

Why this lesson exists
The live course already teaches name and origin. This adds the next natural social question: where the speaker is now. Keep it conversational and low-pressure; the grammar point is only that `joogaa` needs its full ending.

Prerequisites
- Time greetings and wellbeing
- `Magacaygu waa ...`
- `xaggee` from introductions or directions

Core vocabulary
- `joogaa` - am / is staying, am / is located
- `joogtaa` - you are staying / located
- `joognaa` - we are staying / located
- `meesha` - the place
- `magaalada` - the city
- `Hargeysa` - Hargeisa
- `Copenhagen` - Copenhagen
- `adigu` - and you / you yourself

Phrases
- `Meesha aad joogtaa waa?` - Where are you?
- `Xaggee joogtaa?` - Where are you?
- `Waxaan joogaa Hargeysa.` - I am in Hargeisa.
- `Waxaan joogaa Copenhagen.` - I am in Copenhagen.
- `Waxaan joognaa Copenhagen.` - We are in Copenhagen.
- `Adigu xaggee joogtaa?` - And where are you?

Mini-dialogue
- A: `Is ka warran?` - How are you?
- B: `Waan fiicanahay, adiguna?` - I am well, and you?
- A: `Aniguna waan fiicanahay. Xaggee joogtaa?` - I am also well. Where are you?
- B: `Waxaan joogaa Copenhagen. Adigu xaggee joogtaa?` - I am in Copenhagen. And where are you?
- A: `Waxaan joogaa Hargeysa.` - I am in Hargeisa.

Notes for YAML
- Place this after `Introductions` or after `Conversation: First Meeting`.
- Keep this as a conversation lesson with fixed expected replies.
- Reuse `magacaygu waa`, `is ka warran`, and `adiguna` from earlier skills.
- Accept both `Xaggee joogtaa?` and `Meesha aad joogtaa waa?` as question variants if supported.
- Do not add new tense or case teaching here. The learner should finish feeling "I can answer one more real question."
- Challenge mix: mostly conversation turns, with a few chips prompts for `Waxaan joogaa ...` and one short-input item for `joogaa`.
